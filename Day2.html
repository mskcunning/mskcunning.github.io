    Activity 2

    School Network Site Survey

    Walk the building and map the real network infrastructure — wireless access points, cameras, and network closets — the way a technician documents an unfamiliar client site.

    Before you go: stay in your assigned area, don’t touch or unplug any equipment, and let your teacher know your route. You’re documenting what you see, not changing anything.

    Tasks

    What to look for:

    Access point (WAP): usually a small white box or disc on the ceiling with one or two small lights, broadcasting the Wi-Fi.

    Dome camera: a half-sphere behind dark glass, often on a ceiling. Bullet camera: a cylinder pointing in one direction, often on a wall.

    Network closet: a wall rack or small room with switches and bundles of blue/colored cables.

    Extra credit

    Finished early? Pick a challenge

    Anytime after your current activity is submitted

    Only start these once your current activity is submitted and signed off. You can do one challenge or several. Ask your teacher if you’re not sure you’re ready.

    Challenge A: Add a VLAN to your SOHO network

    In Packet Tracer, extend your Activity 4 design so the guest network is separated with a VLAN on the switch instead of just a second SSID. Prove the separation works.

    Challenge B: Wi-Fi channel audit

    Build on your site survey. Use a Wi-Fi analyzer to look at the access points you found and check which channels they use. Spot where neighboring APs overlap on the same channel and recommend better assignments.

    Challenge C: Set up port forwarding

    On your physical SOHO router, set up a port forwarding rule (for example, to a game server or a web server on the desktop PC) and confirm it reaches the right device. Then explain the security trade-off.

    Challenge D: Document the client network

    Write a short handoff document for your SOHO client, the way a real technician would leave one behind, so someone else could manage the network later.
