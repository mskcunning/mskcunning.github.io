    Activity 2

    PC TEMP Chart

    9:15 – 9:50 AM · Teacher-led, we do this together

    Computers are complex systems that require many components and connections to function properly. We’ll fill in the TEMP Chart together as a class. Follow along with your teacher during the presentation and complete each term as we go. Don’t work ahead. TEMP Charts let you define a term and give an example and an image to support it. This chart will come in handy when we build our computers, since you’ll be able to reference the parts you’re using.

    Activity 3

    PC Teardown and Rebuild

    9:50 – 11:07 AM and 11:37 AM – 12:05 PM
    1. Teardown

    Take the PC apart completely, with a junior helping you every step of the way. Ask questions! Learn what each part does and how it comes out.

    2. Bench reconnect

    With your junior’s help, reconnect all the components outside the case on the bench (an “open-air” or “bench test” setup) so you can see how everything connects.

    3. Solo rebuild

    Once the bench test works, rebuild the entire PC inside the case on your own, with no junior help. Show what you’ve learned.

    Extra credit

    Finished early? Pick a challenge

    Anytime after your current activity is submitted

    Only start these once your current activity is submitted. You can do one challenge or all three. Ask your teacher if you’re not sure you’re ready.

    Challenge A: Plan a PC on PCPartPicker

    Go to PCPartPicker and use the System Builder to plan a complete computer. Choose one budget:

    Student PC: under $600

    Fast enough for school work, web browsing, and Minecraft Education.

    Gaming PC: under $1,200

    Smooth gameplay on modern games at 1080p or 1440p.

    Dream build: no limit

    Go all out, but you have to justify every part you pick.

    Challenge B: Upgrade the PC you rebuilt

    Look at the parts in the PC you worked on today. Use PCPartPicker to find three upgrades that would make it faster or better, and make sure each one is compatible with that motherboard.

    Challenge C: Build a giant PC in Minecraft Education

    Open Minecraft Education on the PC you just set up and build a giant model of the inside of a computer. Use signs to label each part.
