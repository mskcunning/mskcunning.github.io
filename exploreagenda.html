    Activity 1

    OSINT Phishing Attack

    Project objectives

    Phishing: the attempt to obtain sensitive information such as usernames, passwords, SSN#, and credit card details, for malicious reasons, by masquerading as a trustworthy entity in an electronic communication.

    Spear-phishing: an email or electronic communications scam targeted towards a specific individual, organization, or business.

    You will use OSINT online search tools to research a target (someone over 18 in your life) to see what information about them is publicly available online.

    Part 1: Online footprint report

    Use OSINT tools to find as much information as possible about your target. Document it with links to websites and screenshots. Try searching for the target’s family members too, since they may have posted useful information.

    Part 2: Phishing email

    Write a phishing email based on your research, aimed at convincing the target to provide some asset: money, credentials, or other items of value.

    Important: Before starting your research, log out of all your social media accounts so information isn’t gathered through your personal account links.

    Information to gather

    Begin with ONLY the target’s name and the town where they live. You may already know some of this, but you must prove you found it online with website links and screenshots.

    Suggested OSINT tools

    You may use any other tools as long as they are publicly accessible.
